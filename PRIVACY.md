# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

## Summary
"My Rclone Backup" is a personal OAuth client used only by the account owner
to transfer files between their own devices and their own Google Drive account
using the rclone command-line tool.

## What data it accesses
The app requests the Google Drive scope (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive).
This allows rclone to read, create, rename, move and delete files only inside
the Google Drive account that authorises it.

## What it does not do
- No data is sold, rented or shared with any third party.
- No analytics, advertising or user tracking of any kind.
- Files are transferred directly between the device and Google Drive.

## Retention and deletion
Access can be revoked at any time from
Google Account > Security > Third-party connections.
Revoking access immediately removes every permission this app has.
